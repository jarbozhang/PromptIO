IMHO, as long as the top AI models remain publicly available to subscribers, there is virtually nothing that can be done to prevent them from being distilled. Turning them into SaaS only available to a few closely vetted companies would also not change matters much. For the better or worse, we have not figured out that many ways of how to effectively monetize software at the scale required for the top labs to keep growing at the current rates of capital expansion.
