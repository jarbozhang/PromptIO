For the record, I never said that AGI would never come! 

- I have said it would very likely come this century (see debate with Grady Booch on my substack)
- i have said that it might well come next decade
- I said pure LLMs wouldn’t get there (they didn’t)
- I said that adding in (neuro)symbolic components would help (they have helped; harnesses, tools, loops, etc)
- I said that neurosymbolic components would be necessary but not sufficient (seems to be true, see my 2020 Next Decade in Arxiv)
- I gave criteria in my essay Dear Elon Musk and my bet with Miles Brundage (both in my substack)

Note also that so far all of these claims have either proved correct or pending but looking promising.  And that receipts abound.
