I connected my calorie tracking app to my @WHOOP and my @wip so now I have some interesting correlations

The worst things for my sleep are (strong correlation)
1) long haul travel (worse than being sick!)
2) being sick
3) short haul travel

Best things for my sleep (weakly correlated):
1) sauna 
2) massage
3) alcohol (?)

Worst things for recovery:
1) being sick
2) alcohol
3) massage (because the lactic acid spreads day after I think)

Other things: tanning lowers my resting heart rate!
