PS nothing wrong with rich kids, I didn't grow up poor at all

My dad was a cardiologist in Netherlands, me and my brothers each had a PC for ex, which was exceptional, not rich like American doctors btw, but about upper middle class in NL

But our parents generally gave us stuff to develop ourselves, not regular toys, but creative stuff like a guitar or PC and it worked out well to develop us

The money mostly stopped around 18 and we had to move out (like most Dutch kids do/did when going to uni) and get a job (I worked in a callcenter because I was DJing/producing drum & bass music) or go uni

I fucked up public high school and parents paid one year private school to get me to finish high school after I failed 3 years and sucked at it so much like my brother

I paid back that money a decade ago 

Anyway I'm happy we grew up well but they cut me off so I could do my own thing and spread my wings and fly
