This is exactly why I hated home automation

I'd check in to a hotel or Airbnb and everything would be controlled with these hideous and unusable touchscreen interfaces

Oddly or not it's always non-tech people who install these and think their house is now modern, but it's hideous

I started liking home automation when I realized you don't need that, and you can have everything work with classic analog buttons while still being automatable in the back with Home Assistant (in my case via KNX)

Same reason everyone hates touchscreens in cars, no feedback
