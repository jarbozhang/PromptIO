A good example of one of the ways in which the "good old days" were terrible:

In the 1880s, the average US rural housewife had to haul water into the house 8-10 times a day, 36 tons of water a year. And also haul out ashes &amp; waste. (From “The Rise and Fall of American Growth”). https://t.co/eKP4XMJVrz
