I have to keep re-subscribing to @Suno cause it keeps getting better

As always not affiliated, not paid, I just like it

I used to make Drum & Bass ages ago professionally so this is very fun to try

Their new model is Suno 5.5 and the coolest thing is they have a Studio, so you generate a song first, then you click Studio, you select your song, you select Split Stems and you get each music layer separate so Drums, Bass, Synth, Vocals etc.

That's when it gets fun cause you can edit the song like you'd make a real song in a DAW (like real music production software)

You can add filters like low pass, high pass etc and much more, I haven't even tried everything, but it's really  fun

Adding the Studio part is great because it adds more human direction to AI sounds, the Suno sounds are now good enough I think, like a Drum & Bass snare sounds punchy and solid, but it still lacks some overall composition/direction which a human can add here

Very cool!!
