To be genuinely useful in our homes and workplaces, robots need finesse.

Gemini Robotics 2 equips different hardware platforms with high dexterity. It can control a five-fingered hand to tie a knot or screw in a lightbulb - while also managing parallel grippers for complex packing tasks.

We’re additionally introducing multi-robot collaboration so completely different types of robots can communicate and work together to solve problems a single robot couldn’t do alone. 🤝
