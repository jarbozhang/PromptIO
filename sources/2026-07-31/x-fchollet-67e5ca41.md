One of the least explored questions in AI is how to build rich open-ended environments for learning. Benchmarks evaluate intelligence, environments shape it.

iLands is interesting because it doesn't just measure agents by how much money they make, it forces them to survive in a living market. Whether that produces qualitatively different forms of adaptation is an open question -- but it’s exactly the kind of experiment the field needs more of.
