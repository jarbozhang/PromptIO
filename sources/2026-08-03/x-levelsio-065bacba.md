Lots of great replies and ideas

One by @marckohlbrugge I added now called [ Fill gap ]

If you have 2 clips and space in between, it will try to generate a video to connect the two in someway and place it after it's done generating

Also added [ Regenerate ] on any clip if you're not happy with the result and it replaces it in place too

And regular [ Generate ] on the timeline lets you generate on any position on the timeline
