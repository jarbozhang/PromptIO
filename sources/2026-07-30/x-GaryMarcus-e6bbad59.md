This post on Anthropic by @dkthomp sounds compelling but I don’t think it actually is.

1. The projections of Anthropic’s revenue are likely overoptimistic. Their big Q2 was projected to be $10.9B (after $4.8B in Q1) but importantly that was at the height of tokenmaxxing, and before strong new competition from Chinese models like Kimi K3 and GLM 5.2, and at moment, now perhaps over, when they were media darlings.  I really don’t see them getting to $100B+ for the year with $85B+ to go, given the new headwinds. 

2. Not really fair to compare to Elon’s companies, which actually aren’t wildly impressive on revenue. Only Tesla is even in the top 100. Even if Anthropic’s revenue *was* $150B, that still would NOT put them in the top 25 US companies by revenue [https://t.co/85bu9d3jaA]. So I find framing the comparison in terms of Elon’s net worth (mostly based on speculation about the future) to be a bit misleading. 

3. The real long-term question is about profits. Anthropic’s best quarter was Q2 of this year, but that relied on a big one-time subsidy for Elon, and again was at the height of tokenmaxxing and before the sharpest Chinese competition. In every other quarter they lost money and it’s not at clear how Q3 will turn out.
