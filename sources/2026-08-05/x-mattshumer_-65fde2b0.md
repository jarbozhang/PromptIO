Part of why I push back is that I think it's quite apparent that things in AI are moving incredibly quickly… and misleading people and telling them that these systems aren't continuing to get more widely capable is dangerous.

I'm not saying I'm 100% right on everything I’ve predicted. We're all human, we're all fallible. We all hallucinate to some extent (just like LLMs :)), but I think directionally you would agree that those of us who believe that this stuff has been moving incredibly quickly and will continue to do so have been more or less right over the long-term.

I'm not a pure optimist by any means. I feel confident that the technology is extremely likely to keep improving at a rapid rate (deep and wide), but I'm not convinced that the overall effects on society and the world as a whole will be positive overall. It’s going to be very mixed (I hope the good will outweigh the bad, but history is not in our favor on this one).

I think it's important that people understand that this is very likely coming, so they have time to think about what it means for them and prepare.
