If you're in marketing, more education makes your income go down: high-school $85K/y -> Bachelor $80K/y -> Master $75K/y

If you're a founder, education does not help or hurt you at all, the change is $0

The only place where education increases income from m data is PhDs who are developers do result in $150K/y median income, but sample size is 50 so it's noisy
