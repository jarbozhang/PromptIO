My take: you can still do it at any age. The important part is to take care of your health first. You need to change your mental template about what a startup should be though. If you are still beholden to the idea of a startup being a bunch of young guys grinding hard in a garage 24/7, you are ngmi. But if you know how to outsource most of your work to AI agents grinding 24/7 in the cloud or your own server room, then you absolutely can have a chance at a successful startup.
