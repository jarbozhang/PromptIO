Artificial intelligence-enabled decision support systems have the potential to transform how military organizations plan, decide, and operate. This report explores practical applications of AI tools in key operational processes and identifies the barriers to broader adoption. The findings highlight the critical role of data accessibility in unlocking AI’s full potential. The report concludes with recommendations for scaling AI capabilities across the force.
