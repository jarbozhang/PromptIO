Unfortunately this doesn’t affect only hotels and restaurants. All businesses and services are like that. When we last moved to a new big city I relied on reviews to find dentists and doctors. I ended up with a completely dud of a dentist, had to go to a different one to correct his “work”. The same holds for Uber drivers, plumbers, etc. It seems that word of mouth and experience are still the most reliable ways of finding good service in most places.
