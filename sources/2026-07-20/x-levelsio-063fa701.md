Great suggestion by @sungamma: "And divide by median time to acquisition of 6 years for annualized earnings above salary"

Median expected outcome for a startup that gets acquired in annualized earnings:

Single founders: ~$4.27 million/year
One co-founder: ~$2.13 million/year
One of 4 co-founders: ~$1.07 million/year

15% odds of getting acquired in annualized earnings:

Single founder: ~$640,000/year
One of two co-founders: ~$320,000/year
One of four co-founders: ~$160,000/year

Oddly close to tech salaries right? That's how expected value in economics works!
