To be clear, @GaryMarcus, your 2023 post did say "some kind of hybrid may well work."

But to be precise about it, for years, what you've meant by "hybrid" is neurosymbolic architecture (some form of symbol manipulation built into the system itself). It did not mean an LLM that's allowed to use a calculator/tools.

This matters because your claim now is that "using tools plus LLMs is what worked." There are two problems with that.

First, in your own words, July 2025, on the IMO gold result: "The fact that no tools, coding or internet was used is genuinely impressive." (Your write-up of that result was fair, by the way... credit where it's due.) So the math breakthrough you're now attributing to tools happened, by your own account at the time, without them. Just scaled deep learning and RL.

Second, even where tools are involved, humans don't do math unaided either. Every mathematician in history used pencil, paper, and eventually computers. "It only counts without tools" is a bar no intelligence has ever cleared.

I'm curious what result would falsify your view. In 2023, failure confirmed LLMs are stuck. In 2026, success confirms the hybrid approach, even though it looks quite different than predicted. If both outcomes count as vindication...
