Hot take: Neural networks are vestiges of the era when we used humans to design and implement ML algorithms. Their composability, modularity, hierarchical nature, and simple implementation in higher level programming languages were helpful when human insight and productivity were the most valuable part of any ML project. That era is coming to the end now.
