He is by far the most famous embodiment of this kind of professor, but the fact is that there was a HANDFUL of such professor at EVERY college I’ve ever taught at. The reason Arday got into trouble was because his confabulations were just a bit too wild even for this cohort, and he aggressively courted wider attention outside of the academia. Other than that, his case is unfortunately way too typical. I know college deans and even university presidents who are as vacuous, but have the good sense of letting their handlers do all the actual work and PR.
