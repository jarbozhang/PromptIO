Got 3x Xiaomi Smart Air Purifier Elite, one for each floor

To be fair they're not the prettiest (round and silver) but they're about the most powerful ones you can get, being able to clean 600 m³/hour for spaces up to 125 m², so essentially one big floor

The best part though is the bigger the air purifier, the more silent they are, you just simply don't hear them, they're on Auto mode and unless the air gets really bad they won't spin up (so in my case they never spin up)

Then I got a small one for the bedroom, the Xiaomi Smart Air Purifier 4, this one's actually just square and white and pretty

Anyway here's the data before and after installing them, AQI went down from 59 in the gym, or about smoking 1 cigarette per day, to 22 (clean air)

P.S. I bought Xiaomi based on the QT thread below, it's great, they actually have their own Home Assistant repo on GitHub which is how every smart home device should be: open and limitless! No bs API limits with 🇨🇳 Xiaomi unlike with 🇯🇵 Daikin (max 150 reqs/day) which tells you all you need to know about the modernity of Chinese tech vs the absolute stoneage levels of Japanese tech!

inb4 "omg Chinese devices phone home" no you can just run these locally on your Home Assistant!

Which is wild cause we live near the ocean in Portugal far from any city and you still have to deal with somewhat dirty air, it's just the reality of modern life. Now imagine what you breath in living in the city?

My aunt died of lung cancer at 55, you never know why but she did live in a big Dutch port city with lots of container ships all day every day exhausting dirty oil fumes, so clean air is a priority for me (another reason I couldn't keep living in Bali and Thailand)

One thing that's still bad is VOC levels (volatile organic compounds), today I learnt you can only get rid of that with carbon filters, no regular HEPA air purifier will help you with that (just like it won't help you with CO2, you just have to open the window or build a fresh air inlet into your house)

Anyway, cleaner air, hooray! 💨
