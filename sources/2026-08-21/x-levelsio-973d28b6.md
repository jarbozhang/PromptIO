🇹🇭 Nomads and expats in Thailand seem to be in the crosshairs of the Thai government and Bank of Thailand this year

Foreigners who have a bank account on a DTV visa are getting them closed, and those trying to open new bank accounts are getting rejected

Also foreigners who own real estate in Thailand through joint ventures with local Thai (a decades long loophole to own real estate where it's actually illegal to own real estate as a foreigner) are now getting forcefully liquidated all over the country
