A lot of what I stand for and tweet about can be boiled down to this: ALL artificial barriers to entry are intrinsically self serving. *SOME* of them could potentially be justified in terms of a greater good that they are trying to create/preserve, but even then they should only be provisional and subject to great critical scrutiny. In the long run, *ALL* of them should be dismantled, as they tend to create unnecessary frictions and inefficiencies, and in the worst case perpetuate some great ethically dubious injustice.
