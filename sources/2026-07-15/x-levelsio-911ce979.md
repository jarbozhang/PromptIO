Many Europeans rent houses, especially in the city, the land lords (esp in Western Europe but also Southern Europe) usually do not allow AC installation outside because they think it's noisy or simple do not like it

Even if you own a home, Europe also has home owner associations (HOAs) that also block AC installation because it looks bad to them

Europe's housing stock is also quite old, so they want to preserve the look (think Paris) which is understandable but there's ways to install it without it even being visible (like in the chimney for ex)

Then there's the massive bureaucracy, for example in many parts of France installing an AC on the outside means you need a permit to change your building which takes ages and will probably be rejected because again they want to preserve the look

So no Europe does not ban AC, but essentially they do make it impossible for most to install AC
